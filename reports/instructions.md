# How to Complete the Weekly Status Report

1. Copy `template.md` or create a new report from it.
2. Enter the reporting period, project or team name, and report owner.
3. Select the overall status: On track, At risk, or Off track.
4. List the most important accomplishments completed during the period.
5. Add measurable progress, such as completed work, milestones, or key metrics.
6. Describe each blocker or risk, its impact, owner, and next action.
7. List the three most important goals for the next reporting period.
8. Record decisions or support needed from managers or stakeholders.
9. Keep bullets concise and focus on information that requires attention.
10. Review the report for accuracy before sharing it.
