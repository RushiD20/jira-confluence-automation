# Jira/Confluence Automation Ideas

## 1. Weekly Project Status Digest

**Problem it solves:** Managers spend significant time collecting progress updates, blockers, and upcoming work from multiple Jira projects and Confluence pages.

**Data it needs:** Jira issue status, assignee, priority, sprint, due date, and comments; Confluence project status pages; and the list of projects or teams to include.

## 2. At-Risk Work Alert

**Problem it solves:** Managers may discover too late that important tasks are overdue, blocked, or likely to miss a milestone.

**Data it needs:** Jira issue status, priority, due date, story points, dependencies, blockers, sprint information, and linked milestone or release dates.

## 3. Automated Decision and Action-Item Tracker

**Problem it solves:** Decisions and follow-up tasks recorded in meeting notes can be missed or remain unassigned after meetings.

**Data it needs:** Confluence meeting pages, decision and action-item text, named owners, due dates, project or team names, and related Jira issue keys.
